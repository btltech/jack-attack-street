Privacy

Jack Attack Street, Ayò Ọlọ́pọ́n, Street Dice and Market Margin are free browser games. There are no accounts, no cookies, no ads and nothing is sold. This page says exactly what the games keep on your device, what they send to our server, and how to switch it off.

Last updated 24 November 2026

The short version

  • ✅ No sign-up, no email, no real name needed.
  • ✅ No cookies, no advertising, no tracking across other websites.
  • ✅ Your progress (stats, coins, themes, streaks) lives on your own device.
  • 📡 The game sends a small amount to our server: the daily leaderboard, online rooms, anonymous usage counts, and streak reminders if you turn them on.
  • 🔕 You can turn the anonymous usage counts off below, and reminders off any time in the game.

Who we are

The games are made and run by BTLTech Ltd (btltech.co.uk), which is responsible for your information under UK data protection law. Questions or requests: info@btltech.co.uk.

Kept on your device only

The games use your browser’s local storage (not cookies) to remember your settings and progress. None of this is sent to us.

  • Jack Attack win/loss stats, coins, power-ups, unlocked themes and your chosen theme, sound and difficulty settings, custom rules.
  • Ayò profile (XP, level, board skin), tutorial progress and mute setting.
  • Your daily-challenge progress and streaks, including today’s Street Dice result. Market Margin saves your daily picks and results in this browser and does not send them to the server; it only sends the anonymous usage counts described below (for example, that you finished the daily and how many best deals you found).

Clearing your browser’s site data for this website removes all of it.

What is sent to our server

WhenWhatWhy
You finish a Jack Attack daily The date, a random guest ID, your nickname, your time and cards played. To rank you on the daily leaderboard. The top 20 nicknames and times are shown publicly.
You finish a Street Dice daily The date, a random guest ID, your nickname, your score and how many rolls you took. Your round-by-round choices are sent so we can check the score, but only the score and roll count are kept. To rank you on the daily leaderboard. The top 20 nicknames and scores are shown publicly.
You play in an online room (either game) Your nickname, a random guest ID, and the moves and cards needed to run the game. To run the match for everyone in the room. We don’t build profiles from it.
You use the games Anonymous usage counts (see below). To learn whether people come back and where they get stuck.
You turn on streak reminders A push address from your browser, the time you’ll be reminded, and your current streaks and last win day. To send you one reminder when a streak is at risk.

Nicknames: they’re shown to other players and on the leaderboard, so please don’t use your real name or anything personal.

Anonymous usage counts

Each event is an event name (like “daily won” or “tutorial finished”), which game, a short detail (like “win” or “normal”), the time, a random ID created in your browser, and the day that browser was first seen. The random ID is separate from your guest ID and is not linked to your nickname, score or reminders. We don’t store your IP address in these records, and we don’t use cookies.

Streak reminders (optional)

If you tap “Remind me”, your browser asks permission and creates a push subscription with its push service (run by Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft, depending on your browser). We store that subscription’s address, the hour you chose (in UTC) and your streaks, and use them to send at most one reminder a day, only when a streak is at risk. The reminder itself contains no personal information. Turning reminders off in the game deletes our copy straight away; we also delete it if your browser’s push service tells us it’s no longer valid. You can also block notifications in your browser’s site settings.

Other companies involved

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and runs our server and database. Like any web host, it processes your IP address to deliver pages and protect against abuse.
  • Google Fonts and Tailwind CSS’s CDN supply the page fonts and styling. Your browser fetches these directly, so those services see your IP address.
  • Your browser’s push service (only if you turn reminders on).

We don’t share your information with advertisers and we don’t sell it.

How long we keep things

  • Leaderboard scores (Jack Attack and Street Dice): kept so past days’ leaderboards still work.
  • Online rooms: used to run the match and not used for anything else.
  • Anonymous usage counts: kept so we can compare weeks and months. They contain no name, email or IP address.
  • Reminder subscriptions: until you turn reminders off or the subscription stops working.

Your choices and rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete information we hold about you, and object to how we use it. Because we don’t know who you are, we can only find your records if you give us an ID: use “Show my anonymous IDs” above and email them to info@btltech.co.uk, and tell us what you’d like done. Our basis for the leaderboard, rooms and anonymous counts is our legitimate interest in running and improving a free game; for reminders it’s your consent, which you can withdraw any time.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled something you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk, though we’d appreciate the chance to put it right first.

The games don’t ask for your age or personal details. If you’re a parent and think a child has put personal information into a nickname, email us and we’ll remove it.

Changes

If what the games collect changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
