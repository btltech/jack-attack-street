How to Play Jack Attack Street

Jack Attack Street is a fast-paced browser card game inspired by classic street Black Jack, Switch, and Crazy Eights. Play solo against smart AI bots, compete on the same-seed Daily Challenge leaderboard, or create an instant online room to challenge friends.

🎯 Basic Rules

  • Each player starts with 7 cards.
  • On your turn, play a card that matches the suit (♥ ♦ ♣ ♠) or rank of the top card on the discard pile — or play a Wild Queen.
  • If you cannot play, tap the draw pile to pick up a card.
  • The first player to empty their hand wins!

⚡ Special Power Cards

💥 2 — Pick Up +2 (Stackable) Forces the next player to draw 2 cards. They can defend and stack another 2 to pass +4, +6, or +8!
🔥 Black Jack (J♠ / J♣) — Mega +5 Forces the next player to draw 5 cards! Only another Black Jack can counter and stack it to +10.
👑 Queen (Q) — Wild Card Play on any card (when not under attack) and declare the next suit in play.
🚫 8 — Skip Skips the next player's turn. In a 2-player game, you go again immediately!
↩️ Ace (A) — Reverse Reverses the turn direction. In a 2-player match, acts like a Skip.
🤴 King (K) — Swap Hands Trade your entire hand with an opponent — the ultimate comeback card!
👁️ 7 — Peek Privately reveals one random card from an opponent's hand.

🌐 Online Rooms & Daily Seed Leaderboard

  • Online with Friends: Tap Online in the lobby, create a room, and drop the invite link in WhatsApp or Discord. No accounts required, and empty seats auto-fill with bots.
  • Daily Seed Challenge: Everyone plays the exact same shuffled deck each day — beat the bot as fast as possible to claim #1 on the global daily leaderboard.
